# Echo Run — Credits & Licences

Game design, code, level design and procedural prop art (spikes, pressure plates, gates,
arrow launchers, arrows, lift trim, rune glyphs, glow/fog/light-shaft textures): original work for Echo Run.

No assets were purchased. Every third-party asset below is public domain (CC0) or under an open licence.

- **Gothicvania Church Pack**: castle tileset, church arch, stone columns, background panels.
  https://opengameart.org/content/gothicvania-church-pack
- **Gothicvania Patreon's Collection**: Gothic Hero (the knight; frames repacked), ghost, night sky/clouds/mountain parallax.
