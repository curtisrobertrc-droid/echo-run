# Echo Run

A cursed knight climbs a ruined citadel. Wherever you fall, an **Echo** of you forms on that exact spot and turns to solid stone. You can stand on it, use it to hold a pressure plate, or hide behind it from arrows. You can also Sacrifice on purpose, which leaves an Echo where you choose. Do it on the ground to kneel, or in mid-jump (new in v1.2) to leave a stone Echo hanging in the air as a floating platform.

- 12 chambers across 3 wings, with star ratings and saves in the browser.
- Plays with a keyboard (arrows/WASD, Space to jump, X or Down to sacrifice, R to restart, Esc to pause) or with touch controls.
- Static HTML5 build: open `index.html`. Phaser is bundled and nothing loads from a CDN.
