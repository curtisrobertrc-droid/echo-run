# Echo Run

A cursed knight climbs a ruined citadel. Wherever you fall, an **Echo** of you forms on that exact spot and turns to solid stone. You can stand on it, use it to hold a pressure plate, or hide behind it from arrows. You can also kneel to Sacrifice on purpose, which leaves an Echo where you choose.

- 12 chambers across 3 wings, with star ratings and saves in the browser.
- Plays with a keyboard (arrows/WASD, Space to jump, X or Down to sacrifice, R to restart, Esc to pause) or with touch controls.
- Static HTML5 build: open `index.html`. Phaser is bundled and nothing loads from a CDN.
